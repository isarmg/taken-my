Sources & confessions

This portrait is made from details your browser shares with the page. These are device hints and settings, not a complete picture of you.

What stays here

The observations stay in this tab’s memory. Activity is counted in totals; no typed words, exact pointer positions, or browsing history are recorded. This app does not save the observations in your browser or upload them. Reloading starts a new portrait. A copy is saved to your device only when you ask; downloaded files stay there until you remove them.

How the page arrives

Your browser downloads the page and its fonts from the host, which may keep ordinary website access logs. Making this portrait sends no additional requests.

The edges of the picture

This page does not retrieve your location, record sound or pictures, read browsing history, or look up your network address. Browser settings and privacy protections may alter what is reported. A hint is not proof of who you are.
